Summary
Situation

HR, IT, and hiring managers tracked new-hire checklists separately across spreadsheets and emails, causing missed Day-1 laptop setups, system access delays, and constant status follow-ups.

Task

Unify HR, IT, and manager onboarding workflows inside one structured relational application with automated task assignment, overdue accountability, and role-appropriate security.

Action

Designed a 4-table 1:N Dataverse schema (Employee, Onboarding Case, Task, Task Template) with Business Rules, role-based Model-Driven views/forms, Business Unit security roles, and Power Automate flows generating template tasks, Teams alerts, Outlook overdue digests, and automatic case closure.

Result

Gave HR, IT, and managers a single source of truth for every hire, prevented missed Day-1 setup tasks, eliminated manual chase emails, and shipped across Dev/Test/Prod Managed Solutions.

Cross-Team Problem & 4-Table Dataverse Schema

Previously, HR emailed start-date details to IT and managers separately, making it easy for equipment or account setup tasks to slip past a new hire's start date. Because onboarding required structured parent-child records and role-based views, Dataverse and a Model-Driven App were chosen over spreadsheets:

  • 4 Relational 1:N Tables: Modeled Employee (1:N Onboarding Case), Onboarding Case (1:N Task), and reusable Task Template definitions seeded into each new case.
  • Dataverse Business Rules: Enforced conditional required fields, due-date offsets, and validation rules directly at the data layer.

Automated Task Generation, Digests & Case Closure

Power Automate removes manual coordination across departments:

  • Auto-Seeded Task Plans: Creating an Onboarding Case automatically generates standard HR, IT, and manager Task rows from active Task Templates with target SLA dates.
  • Teams Alerts & Overdue Digests: Sends immediate Teams task assignments to owners and scheduled Outlook digest summaries highlighting overdue tasks before Day 1.
  • Auto-Close Governance: Automatically marks the parent Onboarding Case complete once every child task reaches Done status.

Role Views, Business Unit RBAC & Simulation

Built for clear ownership across teams without exposing sensitive HR records:

  • Role-Based Views & Security Roles: Configured dedicated HR, IT, and Manager queues paired with Business Unit Dataverse security roles (Employee, Manager, HR).
  • ALM & Handover: Deployed as Managed Solutions (Dev → Test → Prod) with admin checklists and live department training. Inspect the schema in the Dataverse & Copilot Studio Simulator.
