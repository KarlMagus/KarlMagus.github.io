Summary
Situation

Educational institutions and training teams needed objective attendance, engagement, and distraction metrics from recorded lectures without paying proctors to watch hours of footage.

Task

Build an automated video review portal where administrators enroll student photos once, upload class recordings, and review annotated video plus engagement scores.

Action

Sole developer of a Django 5 + Preact/Vite platform integrating AWS Rekognition Video, OpenCV/MoviePy bounding-box compositing, 0–100 attentiveness and sleeping detection heuristics, and DynamoDB/S3 persistence.

Result

Replaced manual lecture auditing with automated student recognition, time-aligned distraction tracking, overlay video playback via secure S3 pre-signed URLs, and downloadable session audit logs.

Stakeholder Problem & Admin Workflow

Training administrators had hundreds of hours of recorded lectures and no practical way to audit attendance or spot disengaged students consistently. The platform turns this into a two-step workflow: admins enroll student reference photos once into AWS Rekognition Face Collections, then upload lecture videos to receive annotated playback and an objective engagement scorecard.

Automated Engagement & Distraction Scoring

Turns raw computer vision signals into actionable classroom metrics:

  • Face Search & Object Grouping: Matches enrolled students at a 75% confidence threshold, tracks 8 discrete emotions, and classifies laptops/notebooks as study items vs. phones/earbuds as distractions.
  • 0–100 Attentiveness Score: Weighted 60% facial emotion confidence and 40% forward eye gaze, deducting points for nearby mobile phone detections.
  • Sleeping & Visitor Tracking: Flags sustained sleeping events (eyes closed ≥ 80% with |Roll| ≥ 45° or |Pitch| ≥ 35°) and uses spatial IoU clustering so unrecognized visitors aren't double-counted.

Video Overlay Rendering, Cloud Storage & Demo

Full-stack delivery and playback infrastructure:

  • Visual Overlay Rendering: Server-side OpenCV and MoviePy pipeline burns green boxes for enrolled students and red boxes for distractions/unrecognized faces into H.264 video for instant review.
  • Secure Media & Telemetry: Stores frame-level events in AWS DynamoDB, relational session audit logs on the backend, and serves S3 videos to the Preact/Vite frontend via 1-hour pre-signed URLs with HTTP Range scrubbing.
  • Interactive Demo: Inspect overlays and test attentiveness scoring in the Interactive Computer Vision Telemetry Demo.

  • Computes objective 0–100 attentiveness scores (60% emotion + 40% forward gaze minus phone penalties)

  • Renders color-coded H.264 overlay video stored on S3 (1-hour pre-signed HTTP Range streaming) with DynamoDB telemetry
