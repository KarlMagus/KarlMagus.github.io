Summary

Platform Overview & Scope

Manual classroom auditing across recorded lectures is labor-intensive and subjective. As sole full-stack developer, I architected an enterprise video intelligence platform leveraging AWS Rekognition Video and OpenCV/MoviePy to automate student face identification, detect classroom distractions, score attentiveness quantitatively, and burn visual overlays.

Asynchronous Vision Pipeline & Scoring Engine

The platform translates raw computer vision signals into actionable pedagogical telemetry:

  • AWS Rekognition Video Orchestration: Parallel asynchronous jobs for student face search (75% confidence threshold against indexed collections), 8 discrete emotions, and object label detection (phones, laptops).
  • Mathematical Attentiveness Formulation: Continuous 0–100 score weighted by facial emotion confidence (60%), forward eye gaze (40%), and active mobile phone proximity penalties.
  • Multi-Factor Sleeping Detection: Sustained heuristic combining closed eyes (confidence ≥ 80%) with abnormal head pose pitch/roll (|Roll| ≥ 45° or |Pitch| ≥ 35°) across temporal windows.
  • Spatial IoU Tracking: Intersection-over-Union spatial clustering to track unrecognized visitors across frames without double-counting.

Compositing Engine, Cloud Persistence & Demo

Full-stack backend architecture and deployment details:

  • Server-Side Overlay Compositing: OpenCV and MoviePy engine burning color-coded bounding boxes (green for enrolled, red for unauthorized/distractions) into H.264 video for instant browser playback.
  • Hybrid Storage Architecture: AWS DynamoDB for telemetry search paired with Django and SQLite for audit logs and session metadata.
  • Secure Media Delivery: S3 pre-signed URLs with dynamic 1-hour expiration and HTTP Range header streaming for buffer-free timeline scrubbing.
  • Interactive Demo: Inspect bounding boxes, scrub time-aligned distraction events, and test scoring formulas in the Interactive Computer Vision Telemetry Demo.
