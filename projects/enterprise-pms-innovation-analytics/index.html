Compelion — Enterprise Strategy & Project Platform

Live enterprise management platform (compelion.com) with Azure MSAL SSO and a 6-level org hierarchy that turns executive briefs into department tasks, tracks SPC metrics, escalates risks, and publishes bilingual insights.

Project TypeCustomer integration
Summary
Situation

Enterprise leadership and PMO teams lacked a unified system connecting high-level strategic initiatives to department tasks, process variance metrics (SPC), and automated blocker escalation—relying on fragmented spreadsheets.

Task

Architect and ship an enterprise project management and analytics platform supporting multi-level org governance, Azure SSO, AI work-breakdown workflows, live collaboration, and bilingual publishing.

Action

Built the Django REST + PostgreSQL backend and React 18/Ant Design frontend with a 6-level org schema, 3 RBAC tiers, Azure MSAL & Google OAuth 2.0, Stripe billing, 5 Gemini planning workflows, SPC + FLAML analytics, Excalidraw/WebSocket collaboration, and an English/Arabic CMS.

Result

Launched live at compelion.com with standardized OpenAPI documentation, giving executives end-to-end visibility from strategic brief to risk-monitored execution and bilingual thought leadership.

Executive Problem & Multi-Tenant Governance (compelion.com)

Enterprise PMOs struggled to keep strategic plans connected to day-to-day department execution. Built and launched at compelion.com, Compelion replaces disconnected spreadsheets with a single multi-tenant workspace secured by Microsoft Azure MSAL and Google OAuth 2.0 SSO plus Stripe subscription billing.

6-Level Org Hierarchy, AI Task Breakdown & Quantitative Analytics

Engineered to match real enterprise governance and operations tracking:

  • Organizational Hierarchy & RBAC: Models 6 nested levels (Organizations → Divisions → Departments → Strategic Themes → Teams → Roles) governed by 3 RBAC tiers (Employees, Admins, Superadmins).
  • 5 Gemini Planning Workflows: Structured AI planning workflows covering Problem Statement, Idea Generator, Task Breakdown, Scorecard, and Executive Report convert brief strategic inputs into structured project milestones, subtasks, and evaluation scorecards.
  • SPC, Forecasting & Risk Escalation: Computes Statistical Process Control (SPC) charts, Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA), FLAML AutoML forecasts, team workload/sentiment metrics, and a risk matrix that automatically escalates critical blockers up departments.

Real-Time Collaboration & Bilingual (EN/AR) Publishing

Cross-team communication and content workflows inside the same platform:

  • Live Workspaces: Multi-room chat built on Django Channels WebSockets alongside embedded Excalidraw whiteboards and ApexCharts / Highcharts / Plotly.js / Mermaid.js visuals.
  • Bilingual CMS: English and Arabic publishing workflow using Gemini to draft insights articles, OpenAI DALL-E 3 for header artwork, and attached audio podcasts, backed by standardized OpenAPI documentation.
