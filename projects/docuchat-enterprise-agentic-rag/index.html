DocuChat AI — Contract & Policy Assistant

Private on-premise multi-document Q&A workspace that lets legal and HR teams cross-examine 200+ page contracts and policy manuals in seconds—returning exact document names and page citations.

FocusOn-premise multi-contract RAG, verifiable page/filename citations, and FastAPI/Streamlit + Django deployments.
Core TechnologiesChromaDB · Ollama · Sentence Transformers · FastAPI
Project TypeLLM applications
Summary
Situation

Legal, HR, and compliance teams spent hours manually searching across 200+ page policy manuals and vendor contracts, unable to upload confidential legal documents to external cloud chatbots or trust unverified AI summaries.

Task

Build an on-premise document Q&A system supporting multi-contract comparison and strict page-level citations for fast audit verification.

Action

Built a PyPDF2 section/page metadata chunker, local all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings in ChromaDB, local Ollama (Llama 3.1, Mistral) inference with Pydantic citation contracts, and packaged both FastAPI + Streamlit and multi-user Django deployments.

Result

Cut policy and contract clause lookup from hours to seconds across 200+ page PDFs while keeping 100% of legal data on local infrastructure with verifiable document-and-page citations and saved audit bookmarks.

Stakeholder Need: Fast, Auditable & Strictly Private Search

HR and legal teams needed to compare vendor SLAs, termination clauses, and internal policies across thick 200+ page PDFs without violating client confidentiality by sending agreements to public LLM providers—and without wasting time double-checking vague AI summaries.

Verifiable Citations & Multi-Document Retrieval

Engineered for trustworthy compliance workflows:

  • Metadata-Rich Ingestion: PyPDF2 parses overlapping text chunks while preserving exact section headings and page numbers on every chunk stored in persistent ChromaDB collections.
  • Cross-Contract Selection: Users filter and query across multiple uploaded documents in a single question to compare obligations side by side.
  • Enforced Source Attribution: Local embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) and local Ollama models (Llama 3.1, Mistral) output Pydantic-validated responses listing the source filename and page number for every claim.

Two Ready-to-Deploy Workspaces

Shipped in two configurations depending on client team size:

  • Lightweight Mode (FastAPI + Streamlit): Rapid single-team local workspace for immediate PDF indexing and multi-document Q&A.
  • Enterprise Multi-User Mode (Django + PostgreSQL/SQLite): Full user authentication, persistent conversation history, and saved clause bookmarks for ongoing legal audits.

Architecture Stack

Engineering Highlights

  • Private local execution: answers complex queries across 200+ page HR manuals and legal contracts with zero cloud API uploads
  • Multi-document cross-examination: select multiple PDFs simultaneously to compare clauses side-by-side in one query
  • Pydantic-enforced citation schema requires every response to show exact source document filenames and page numbers
  • PyPDF2 overlapping chunker preserves section headings and page numbers in persistent ChromaDB metadata
  • Delivered two deployment options: lightweight FastAPI + Streamlit app and multi-user Django app with saved bookmarks
