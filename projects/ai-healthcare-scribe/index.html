Voice AI Front Desk & EHR

An autonomous voice AI front desk and central healthcare record architecture for hospitals and clinics, handling telephony bookings, doctor consultations, lab loops, and FHIR R4 interoperability.

FocusVoice AI conversational telephony, healthcare interoperability (FHIR R4), multi-tenant EHR architecture, and privacy governance.
Core TechnologiesElevenLabs · FastAPI · n8n · FHIR R4
Project TypeLLM applications
Summary
Situation

Hospitals and specialty clinics face high front-desk phone congestion and missed booking requests, while disjointed diagnostic lab reporting delays critical doctor follow-ups and patient treatment.

Task

Architect and build an end-to-end voice AI front desk and central electronic health record (EHR) platform capable of autonomous conversational booking, doctor consultation workflows, and lab report loop closure.

Action

Built inbound voice agent on ElevenLabs SIP telephony with deterministic slot reservation tools; architected swappable EHR/HIS adapters; orchestrated WhatsApp notifications and lab report alerts using n8n; implemented FHIR R4 canonical schemas and restricted diagnostic center report portals.

Result

Delivered working architecture and closed-loop prototype from telephony booking through lab report ingestion, implementing deterministic slot verification, emergency triage guardrails, and DPDP/HIPAA-aligned privacy controls.

Platform Overview & Architecture Modes

Bridges conversational voice agents with hospital information infrastructure through a strict architectural principle: the voice agent talks, the backend decides. ElevenLabs manages natural dialogue and multilingual switching, while deterministic backend APIs execute doctor slot availability, patient identification, and transactional bookings without exposing database models to LLM hallucination.

  • Standalone Deployment (Mode A): Complete native multi-tenant EHR stack (scheduling, clinical records, doctor portal, lab portal, voice agent) for clinics without legacy systems.
  • Integrated HIS Deployment (Mode B): Connects into existing hospital information systems via swappable adapters (FHIR R4 REST, HL7 v2, database views) with write-through booking idempotency.
  • Hybrid Architecture (Mode C): High-throughput voice telephony and diagnostic ordering managed natively while mirroring patient masters to enterprise hospital databases.

Conversational Telephony & Clinical Workflows

The platform orchestrates the complete patient care journey across four integrated touchpoints:

  • Inbound Conversational Front Desk: Ingests patient calls over SIP trunks with mandatory AI disclosure, resolves shared family phone numbers via multi-factor matching (phone, name, DOB), checks doctor slots, holds reservations, and dispatches WhatsApp/SMS confirmations.
  • Clinical Refusal & Emergency Triaging: Deterministic refusal guardrails detect acute medical emergencies (chest pain, trauma) to trigger instant emergency directives and warm transfers to hospital ops staff.
  • Doctor Consultation Portal: Clinicians review patient history, previous lab reports, and AI call transcripts, recording ICD diagnoses and issuing structured diagnostic orders with unique order IDs.
  • Minimal-Access Diagnostic Lab Portal: Diagnostic centers access operational order details with zero exposure to confidential doctor notes, uploading finalized reports to close the clinical loop.

Workflow Automation & Privacy Governance

Built to adhere to healthcare regulatory standards and data protection mandates:

  • Workflow Automation with n8n: Event-driven pipelines handle WhatsApp booking confirmations, payment link generation, post-call summary ingestion, and instant clinician alerts when diagnostic reports are submitted.
  • HL7 FHIR R4 Canonical Modeling: Core schemas natively model Patient, Practitioner, Slot, Appointment, ServiceRequest, and DiagnosticReport, providing an extensible foundation for ABDM (ABHA M1/M2).
  • Privacy Governance & Conformance: Purpose-specific consent capture logged with timestamp and audio verification hash, field-level RBAC, clinical note encryption, signed short-lived AWS S3 URLs, and TRAI-compliant regulated calling windows (9 AM–9 PM).
