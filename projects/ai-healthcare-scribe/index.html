CareLoop — AI Clinic Receptionist & Portal

Automates 24/7 clinic phone bookings, WhatsApp confirmations, and diagnostic lab report alerts—connecting an AI voice front desk to role-based Doctor, Lab, and Operations portals.

Focus24/7 patient phone scheduling, multi-portal clinic workflow automation, n8n WhatsApp integration, and HL7 FHIR R4.
Core TechnologiesElevenLabs · FastAPI · n8n · HL7 FHIR R4
Project TypeLLM applications
Summary
Situation

Specialty clinics lose patients to busy reception phone lines while receptionists spend hours sending manual WhatsApp reminders and doctors waste consultation time chasing missing diagnostic lab reports.

Task

Automate inbound patient scheduling, WhatsApp confirmations, and lab-to-doctor follow-up workflows across patients, doctors, and diagnostic labs.

Action

Connected ElevenLabs Voice AI over SIP telephony to FastAPI slot-booking endpoints with multi-factor verification and emergency call transfers, orchestrated n8n WhatsApp/report webhooks, and built 3 React/Next.js portals on HL7 FHIR R4 + PostgreSQL RLS.

Result

Delivered an integrated clinic operations platform eliminating front-desk call bottlenecks, automating post-call WhatsApp confirmations, restricting lab staff away from private clinical notes, and closing the follow-up loop automatically.

Stakeholder Problem & End-to-End Clinic Workflow

Clinics face three daily bottlenecks: missed patient booking calls, manual WhatsApp payment/appointment reminders, and delayed doctor reviews when diagnostic labs send PDFs over email or WhatsApp. CareLoop streamlines all three stakeholder journeys in one system:

  • Patients: Call the clinic 24/7, switch languages naturally, verify identity via phone + name + DOB (resolving shared family numbers), confirm real doctor slots, and receive instant WhatsApp confirmation and payment links.
  • Doctors: View daily schedules, AI call transcripts, prior lab results, and clinical history in the Doctor Portal, and issue structured lab orders in one click.
  • Diagnostic Labs & Ops: Labs log into a restricted portal showing only patient ID, test name, and ordering doctor (hiding private clinical notes) to upload PDF reports and biomarkers, immediately triggering doctor alerts.

Voice Scheduling, Safety Triage & n8n Automation

Engineered to protect clinical safety and eliminate manual front-desk coordination:

  • Telephony Tool Integration: ElevenLabs SIP voice agent invokes FastAPI endpoints to query live doctor availability, place temporary database locks on slots, and commit verified appointments.
  • Emergency Symptom Triage: Keyword and intent safety rules recognize acute symptoms (chest pain, trauma, breathing difficulty) and transfer the call straight to human clinic staff.
  • Webhook-Driven n8n Pipelines: Automatically dispatch WhatsApp confirmations, payment links, post-call summaries, doctor notifications upon lab upload, and outbound follow-up booking calls.

HL7 FHIR R4 Interoperability & Data Governance

Built for both standalone clinics and existing hospital deployments:

  • HL7 FHIR R4 Canonical Schema: Models Patient, Practitioner, Slot, Appointment, ServiceRequest, DiagnosticReport, and Encounter with adapter interfaces for FHIR REST or HL7 v2 hospital systems.
  • Access Governance: Protected with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), role-scoped UI views, short-lived AWS S3 signed URLs for medical PDFs, and containerized Docker deployment.
