Summary
Situation

HR and IT service-desk staff fielded repetitive Teams messages and emails asking questions already documented across internal policy PDFs, while leadership strictly prohibited ungrounded AI answers.

Task

Deploy a Teams chatbot that answers recurring policy questions accurately from approved documents only, refuses to hallucinate on missing topics, and escalates uncovered edge cases seamlessly.

Action

Built 15–20 Copilot Studio topics grounded strictly in approved SharePoint HR/IT libraries, configured explicit no-source refusal behavior, wired a Power Automate flow to raise service-desk tickets with transcript context, and tuned topics from unanswered query logs post-launch.

Result

Gave employees fast 24/7 answers inside Microsoft Teams, diverted repeat policy pings away from HR/IT, prevented unverified policy fabrication, and created a measurable backlog of missing documentation.

Stakeholder Goal: Fast Teams Help Without Hallucinations

Employees rarely knew which SharePoint folder held the latest HR or IT guide, so they defaulted to messaging service-desk teams directly in Microsoft Teams. At the same time, HR and compliance stakeholders needed a hard rule: the bot could never guess or invent company policy.

  • 15–20 High-Frequency Topics: Structured dedicated conversation paths for common requests such as leave balances, travel receipt limits, onboarding equipment requests, and access resets.
  • Strict SharePoint Knowledge Scope: Connected generative answers strictly to approved SharePoint HR/IT policy folders while blocking open-web fallbacks.

Power Automate Service-Desk Ticket Handoff

When an employee asks about an edge case not covered by approved documentation, the agent bridges cleanly into human support:

  • Explicit Refusal Guardrail: Informs the staff member clearly when approved documents do not cover the rule instead of synthesizing an uncertain answer.
  • One-Click Ticket Flow: Calls a Power Automate flow under Service Account credentials to log a service-desk ticket containing the employee's identity, question summary, and chat context.

Post-Launch Tuning & Interactive Simulator

Sustained accuracy through real usage telemetry and structured handover:

  • Continuous Topic Tuning: Reviewed unanswered query logs after rollout to spot missing synonyms, update SharePoint SOP wording, and add new topics where patterns emerged.
  • Interactive Demo: Try grounded Q&A vs. guardrail fallback in Tab 3 of the Power Platform & Copilot Studio Simulator.
