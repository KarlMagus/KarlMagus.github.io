Summary
Situation

A mid-size services company (~400 staff) handled leave and expense requests over email threads and a shared Excel workbook, causing lost approvals, zero status visibility, and hours of manual HR reconciliation.

Task

Replace the ad-hoc email/Excel process with a governed self-service request portal, enforceable manager approvals, role-scoped permissions, and leadership turnaround reporting.

Action

Modeled 5 SharePoint Lists with Lookup/Choice columns and upfront 5,000-item view indexing (Status, Employee, Created), built a role-based Canvas App with pre-submit duplicate checks, automated Teams/Outlook approvals with 48-hour reminders and escalations under Service Account Connection References, and added a Power BI Teams tab.

Result

Cut average approval turnaround from days to ~1 day, eliminated manual HR reconciliation, kept requests viewable strictly by requester, manager, and HR, and handed over user guides, admin checklists, and trained champions.

Stakeholder Bottleneck & 5-List SharePoint Data Design

Inside the client's Microsoft 365 tenant, roughly 400 employees across multiple departments submitted leave and expense requests by email, leaving HR to reconcile approvals manually in Excel. Starting data-first before building screens ensured long-term SharePoint scalability:

  • 5 Normalized Lists: Structured Requests (~20 columns), Employees, Departments, Request Types, and an append-only Audit/Log list linked via Lookup and Choice columns (Status, Type).
  • Upfront 5,000-Item Indexing: Indexed Status, Employee, and Created at launch so filtered galleries and OData flow queries remain delegation-safe past SharePoint's 5,000-item threshold.
  • Pre-Submit Guardrails: Canvas App validation prevents missing receipts, invalid date ranges, and duplicate submissions before creating records.

Multi-Stage Teams Approvals & Try/Catch Resilience

Power Automate orchestrates the full lifecycle from submission to audit logging:

  • Teams & Outlook Actionable Approvals: On submission, the flow sets Status = Pending and sends a Teams Adaptive Card plus an Outlook actionable email to the employee's line manager.
  • 48-Hour Reminder & Escalation: Sends a reminder after 48 hours of inactivity and escalates overdue approvals to the next-level manager listed on Departments.
  • Service Account Governance & Try/Catch: Runs via a Service Account and Connection References (preventing broken flows when staff leave) wrapped in Try/Catch scopes that email admins direct flow run links on failure.

Entra Security Groups, Power BI & Interactive Demo

Delivered inside Managed Solutions (Dev → Test → Prod) with clear access boundaries and visibility:

  • Role-Based Access: Enforced Employee, Manager, and HR Entra ID security groups with item-level SharePoint permissions restricting sensitive items to the requester, approver, and HR.
  • Teams Power BI Reporting: Published a Power BI report inside a Microsoft Teams tab tracking request counts, pending approvals per manager, and approval turnaround (reduced from days to ~1 day).
  • Interactive Demo: Inspect the 5-list schema and simulate approvals or Try/Catch errors in the SharePoint & Approval Flow Simulator.
