Executive LinkedIn & Podcast Automation

Eliminates 10+ hours/week of manual executive publishing by automating research, pre-building a 5-day bilingual queue of LinkedIn posts, chart-backed articles, and two-speaker podcasts, and handling follow-up comments.

FocusResearch-backed bilingual drafting, 5-day Django-Q2 lookahead, LinkedIn OAuth, and podcast/newsletter workflows.
Core TechnologiesDjango 3.2 · OpenAI Responses API · PyAutoGen · Django-Q2
Project TypeCustomer integration
Summary
Situation

An executive consulting team spent over 10 hours a week researching industry topics, writing bilingual (English and Arabic) LinkedIn posts and deep articles, recording audio, and manually replying to comments.

Task

Build one Django operations console that pre-drafts a weekly bilingual content package (posts, research articles, charts, podcasts, newsletters) and automates official-API publishing and engagement.

Action

Engineered a multi-agent research pipeline (OpenAI Responses web search, BeautifulSoup scraping, ChromaDB, PyAutoGen), a 5-day Django-Q2 midnight queue, PyDub dual-speaker TTS podcasts, Gmail newsletters, LinkedIn OAuth publishing, and duplicate-guarded 3-step comment workflows.

Result

Operators simply review and approve pre-generated bilingual drafts in one dashboard while keeping scheduled publishing, blog cross-links, comment replies, and email newsletters synced on one database-backed system.

Stakeholder Bottleneck & Editorial Workflow

Executive marketing teams lose entire workdays coordinating separate tools for research, bilingual copywriting (English and Arabic), data charts, podcast recording, and LinkedIn community management. Built for a consulting publishing team, this Django console brings the entire weekly content workflow into one operator dashboard:

  • Posts & Images: Drafts English and Arabic LinkedIn posts tailored to weekly weekday topics with Pillow + OpenAI generated banners for personal or company pages.
  • Rolling 5-Day Lookahead Queue: A daily midnight job checks today plus four days ahead and pre-populates empty slots so editorial teams always review ready-to-publish drafts instead of starting from a blank page.
  • Podcasts & Newsletters: Converts finished articles or uploaded PDF and Word documents into alternating male/female two-speaker podcasts in English or Arabic and emails subscriber lists via Gmail API with signed unsubscribe links.

Research Article & Multi-Agent Pipeline

Multi-agent editorial orchestration pairs structured OpenAI Responses pipelines with selective PyAutoGen helper agents:

  • Live Web & Consulting Insights Search: Gathers web sources alongside BeautifulSoup4 scraping of consulting-insights portals and ChromaDB context.
  • Structured Section & Chart Planning: Plans titles and sections, builds interactive ApexCharts figures and Mermaid.js diagrams where needed, and stores bilingual English/Arabic versions on one record.

Queue Reliability & Official LinkedIn API Constraints

Designed for predictable operations without extra broker overhead:

  • Database-Backed Django-Q2 Queue: Uses the main relational database instead of requiring the client team to operate a separate Redis service.
  • OAuth 2.0 & Comment Chains: Monitors 60-day LinkedIn token expirations before failure. After publishing, triggers a 3-comment chain (technical point, relevant link to the client's compelion.com blog API, and CTA) plus jitter-spaced replies using API member-mention tags and answered-ID deduplication.

Engineering Highlights

  • Pre-builds a 5-day queue of English/Arabic LinkedIn posts, research articles, and images every midnight via Django-Q2
  • Multi-agent research pipeline combines OpenAI web search, consulting-insights scraping, ChromaDB, and PyAutoGen agents with charts/diagrams
  • Generates dual-speaker English/Arabic podcasts (OpenAI TTS + PyDub) from articles or uploaded PDF/Word documents and sends Gmail newsletters
  • LinkedIn OAuth 2.0 (personal & company pages, 60-day token health alerts) with human-in-the-loop review before publish
  • Automates a 3-comment post-publish chain (linking client blog compelion.com) and author-mention comment replies with duplicate/jitter guards
