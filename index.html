About Me

Hi, I'm Ishraq Ahmad, an AI/ML Engineer in New Delhi, with more than 2 years of experience building and deploying production machine learning and Generative AI systems. I work the full lifecycle, from model development, training, and evaluation through backend engineering, deployment, and production infrastructure, and I take ownership from understanding requirements and designing the solution to implementation, deployment, and delivery. I work closely with clients and stakeholders to turn their ideas and requirements into end-to-end systems, from solution discovery to final deployment and realized value, and I step outside my own discipline when the overall solution needs it.

I'm a natural problem solver, at ease with tools I already know and with learning new fields. I speak English, Hindi, and Urdu, and I translate technical concepts for business stakeholders. I have a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Jamia Hamdard University and currently work as a Machine Learning Engineer. In my free time I read about new developments and experiment with new technologies and approaches, looking for emerging opportunities and how to apply them in the real world.
