About Me

Hi, I'm Ishraq Ahmad, an AI/ML Engineer based in New Delhi, with 2+ years of experience building and deploying production machine learning and Generative AI systems. My work spans across the full lifecycle of AI development and deployment, ranging from model development, training, and evaluation, to backend engineering, deployment, and production infrastructure, with a focus on realizing the ideas and requirements of business partners.

I'm a natural problem solver who enjoys both working on a technology I'm already familiar with and learning new fields. I'm confident when working in multiple areas of AI/ML development, ranging from ML model development, including LLMs, deployment, and operations, and have the ability to step outside of my discipline and contribute to the overall solution, when the situation requires. My expertise ranges from RAG pipelines, LLM application development, Agentic AI, computer vision, and full-stack AI application development with proficiency in software engineering fundamentals, including Python, FastAPI/Django, and cloud technologies like AWS, GCP, and Azure. I work closely with clients and stakeholders to understand their requirements and build end-to-end AI/ML solutions that fulfill their needs, from solution discovery to final deployment and realization of value.

Besides being fluent in English, Hindi, and Urdu, I also have strong communication skills and can effectively translate complex technical concepts to business stakeholders. In my free time, I like to read about the latest tech developments and experiment with new technologies and approaches to problem-solving. I'm always looking to understand emerging opportunities and how to best apply them in the real world.

I have a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Jamia Hamdard University and currently work as a Machine Learning Engineer, contributing to multiple projects for different clients. I take ownership of my work across the development lifecycle, from understanding requirements and designing solutions to implementation, deployment, and delivery.
