About Me

Hi, I'm Ishraq Ahmad. I'm an AI & Solutions Engineer based in New Delhi with nearly 3 years of experience taking business applications and AI automations from initial stakeholder conversations to production. Working directly with founders, executives, and operations teams across the world(including US, UK, UAE, and Canada), I stay involved across the entire application lifecycle: understanding the business workflow, building and deploying the software, writing plain-language user guides and setup runbooks, leading live handover walkthroughs for non-technical teams, and supporting continuous optimization after launch.

Whether a team needs a custom LLM agent, a full-stack web platform, or a business process automated on the Microsoft Enterprise Platform (Power Automate, Power Apps, Copilot), I focus on the operational outcome stakeholders are paying for which includes saving manual hours, removing bottlenecks, and shipping reliable tools that teams actually adopt. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Jamia Hamdard University and speak English, Hindi, and Urdu.
